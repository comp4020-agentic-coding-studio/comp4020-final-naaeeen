Do not add exact player totals, microwave mechanisms, removal-triggered deaths,
rescue procedures or institutional responses to this packet without additional
verified sources. The official summaries are evidence of the adaptations' framing,
not survivor accounts or independent corroboration of one another. The source
guide owns short original paraphrases; other pages link to it rather than copying
long passages. Quotes require exact wording and a location.

## Teaching alignment

The source ledger supports three actions: classify a claim, compare perspectives,
and justify an argument while naming missing evidence. Students practise these
in the seminar and submit a short argument; the Source comparison assessment
uses the same skills with a longer, more carefully cited response. This follows
[CMU Eberly's alignment guidance](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/basics/alignment.html).
Our choice of a fictional history, source packet and activity sequence is a
course-design decision, not a learning-outcome claim established by that guidance.

The lecture should explain a method with a worked example rather than repeat the
packet. The deck should cue discussion; the lecture notes preserve the explanation
in readable HTML. The assignment's final twelve-week coherence remains a later
acceptance question, not something one unit can prove.
