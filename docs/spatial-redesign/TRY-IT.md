# Try the neighbourhood

Open [http://localhost:4093](http://localhost:4093). This address works only on the
computer running the preview.

## Bring a housemate

Choose **Create house**, enter your name and select **Bedrooms**: 2–6 permanent places,
including yours. Use **⌂** (**Invite and house settings**) → **Copy code**.
Share the entire eight-character **House code**; the other identity chooses
**Join friends** → **Join house**. Returning residents use **Continue saved home**.

Use separate browser profiles, different browsers, or a regular window plus an
incognito/private session for distinct identities. Same-profile tabs share an
identity; observer tabs offer **Control from this tab** to take over.
Windows in one private session also share an identity.

Move with WASD/arrows and **E**, or **Movement** on touch screens. **Places** offers
routes; **Quiet** and **Can chat** express availability.

## Make a room yours

Choose **My room** → **Walk to my room**. Furniture, palette and lighting have
separate **Save layout**, **Save room settings** and **Save lighting** actions.

Inside, open **Room studio** or interact with **Reference desk**. Use **Choose one
PNG** → **Save chosen PNG**. The prepared copy is bounded to one megapixel and
256 KiB. Pan/zoom, then **Save this view** to retain your crop.
Your saved reference stays visible after closing.

Choose a **Room preset**, then **Save room preset**. **Listen here** starts local
sound; **My volume**, **Mute here** and **Stop listening** affect only you.
**Look around**/closing stops sound; visits start silent.

Enable **Open my room to current housemates**, then **Save room
settings**. Housemates use **Places** → **Visit a resident** → **Visit [name] · door
open**. Closed doors block visits. Visitors inspect the
owner’s reference; their pan/zoom stays local. Closing your door ends room access.

## Inspect together at the sofa

Choose **Places** → **Rest corner**, sit, then explicitly **Join**. **Share my room
reference with house** publishes a temporary house-visible copy, even if your
room’s door closes later. **Present shared image** resumes the last shared crop.
Others choose **Join**, then **Follow presenter**.

The presenter can pan/zoom and click/tap the image to point. Followers use
**Stop following** or pan/zoom independently. **Leave gathering** or **Leave shared
view · keep seat** ends participation while keeping the chair. The shared copy
clears when everyone leaves.

## Study and draw

**Places** → **Study seats** leads to real chairs. In **Study huddle**, **Join** is
voluntary; **Lead huddle** names the volunteer explaining. **Open independent
board** and **Follow huddle · open board** open the full board while retaining the
huddle at your seat. Board edits are collaborative; huddles do not synchronise
viewports.

**Shared board** also works without joining a huddle. Its companion **House chat**
is separate from lounge/bedroom **Chat**. Use **Return to house** to return.

## Take a game break

Choose **Places** → **Break table · choose an activity**. Sit in a free game chair
and wait for its choices. Sitting starts you watching. In **Picture table**, choose
**Start picture** if needed, then **Join picture** separately. Up to the house’s
capacity can join: six people need a six-place house and six occupied game chairs.
Choose a loose piece, then a picture place; **Previous**/**Next** page the tray.
**Four-in-a-row** remains a two-player activity.

**Players & options** contains all names and **Return to my room**. **Leave picture**
ends participation but keeps your chair. **Stand / leave chair and activity**
releases both. **Look around** keeps game participation and the chair;
**Table choices** reopens the panel.

Confirmed room/studio saves, board edits and picture progress persist. Find study
cards at **Options** → **Saved work**, and personal exports at **House settings** →
**Download my saved data**. Lounge/bedroom chat keeps 100 messages for up to seven
days. Gatherings, following and listening are temporary; missing replies belong in
**Options** → **Pending drafts**.

## If a chair is still confirming

Wait for the seated pose before choosing another chair. **Stand / leave chair and
activity** is the explicit way to get up. If the connection cannot confirm a chair,
use **Options** → **Return to title** → **Continue saved home** to re-enter. A
pending chair does not automatically replay a Join or claim another seat.
