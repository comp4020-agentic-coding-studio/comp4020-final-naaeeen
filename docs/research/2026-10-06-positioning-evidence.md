No cited study directly tests this2-6avatar house or proves grades,productivity or
wellbeing effects. Implications are hypotheses. Parent full-text reading was
possible for Portholes/place theory; other access levels are stated rather than
silently inflated.

## Existing technical evidence and constraints

Frozen Crit8 remains6dc79c5; current branch baseline5c774ae has local planning
and isolated spikes. The [earlier record](../planning/COMPARISON-AND-REVIEW.md)
shows local geometry/transport tests,not an integrated product or demand evidence.
Six synthetic clients over20seconds on one host do not establish whole-game
Fly performance or repeat use. Native browser layout/fixtures do not establish
human delight. No technical trial is repeated merely for a larger count.

Retain2-6house code,owned DIY rooms/doors,moving avatars,actual text,presence,
shared study and useful board as owner constraints. Timer,pen tools,uploads,
unlimited furniture,full history-management UI and public discovery are design
choices that must earn cost. Safety/identity/privacy cannot be dropped when those
boundaries are exposed,but lifecycle mechanisms and user-facing feature menus
should not be confused.

[Final brief](https://comp.anu.edu.au/courses/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/assessments/final-project/)
values a reasoned definition of good and the agreement between README,rules,checks
and actual use; extra unrelated features are not a grading advantage.
[Assessment HD rubric](https://comp.anu.edu.au/courses/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/topics/assessment/)
requires a focused response,corroborated decision/correction evidence and robustness
under use. Neither subagent agreement nor a large bibliography guarantees HD.

The owner asks one specific purpose and user goal,with delivery by modules and
phases. A candidate must state target/trigger/one outcome,feature-to-purpose role,
fair baseline,what would falsify its value,and executable budget gates.
