3. Admit contributors 4, 5 and 6 only after progress is three. With that person still absent, contributor 3 places another piece. Durable progress remains and increments. Contributor 2 explicitly rejoins, obtains a different participation UUID, and places another piece. Prior placements remain exactly attributed.
5. Reload contributor 3, producing a real socket/page disconnect. Before Continue, the other peers' roster must drop to five without losing durable progress. No automatic new Start/Join is performed.
