# Owner redesign brief, 7 October 2026

The owner tried the local app and found the overall UI/UX poor. They request a
coherent game-style opening screen, HUD and settings, better room/avatar/furniture
proportions with room for future content, and substantially improved camera tracking.
Other games and spatial web apps should inform the design. Passing earlier tests
is not evidence that the owner accepted the interaction quality.

The shared board is now a full workspace: opening it presents a whiteboard with
movable companion text chat. It supports drawing, sticky notes, written notes and
paste. It must also work directly as an independent web app without loading the
3D game. Treat it as a useful product in its own right. Preserve real multiuser
sharing and saved return rather than making a decorative editor demo.

The earlier card-only cut line and drawing-as-future restriction are superseded
for this phase. Existing study cards and identity/room/DIY/chat/recovery permissions
remain functional. The owner clarified the unfinished final phrase was a typo;
there is no additional requirement from that phrase.

All delivered UI, source and documents remain English. Explore sources in any
language. Work in the WSL final repository, use the active harness/research methods,
record useful comparisons and fresh reviews per meaningful subsection, critically
verify findings, refine and make logical local commits. No new publication or paid
licence acquisition is inferred. Original Crit 8 and legacy data remain preserved.
