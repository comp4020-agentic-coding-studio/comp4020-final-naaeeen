For substantial research, planning, design and implementation, use an evidence-driven workflow at each phase, section and meaningful subsection. Revisit applicable research/method files at entry, after changed assumptions or findings, and before completion. Explore broadly across languages and sources; verify important claims against current primary sources and actual artifacts.
Use comparisons and relevant skills/techniques frequently. For material choices with credible alternatives, define the same task, acceptance criteria and stopping condition before a controlled comparison. Use isolated matched A/B for consequential agent/prompt/harness choices where available. Require independent reviews without previous chat history or earlier verdicts for consequential plans/changes when supported; provide sufficient factual context. Critically verify findings yourself, refine, then recheck affected paths. Never automatically trust skills, subagents, tests or the first answer.
Keep granular evidence and useful handoffs. Distinguish technical comparisons, model judgement, browser/device checks and real-user A/B tests; never invent trials or turn proposed checks into results. Use techniques as often as they improve outcomes, without endless green-check repetition or artificial experiments for trivial requests. Preserve existing user/project constraints and authorization. Use available tools; report material gaps honestly and continue independent authorized work.
