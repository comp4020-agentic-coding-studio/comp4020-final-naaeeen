# ADR: share the explanation, preserve the learner's statement

Date: 12 October 2026.
Status: retrospective implemented-behavior record, recommendation reaffirmed by
new source review and controlled comparison. Implementation predates the comparison.
Student discussion acceptance remains their judgement.

## Context

A two-to-six-person house supports familiar peers studying individually and
occasionally helping one another. The owner expanded the board into a full
workspace. Sharing a canvas does not determine who may state a learner's goal
or end their request.

## Decision

Current members edit canvas objects. Each author controls their separate card's
goal/question/nextStep/helpRequested and closure. The durable authority checks
house/identity/ownership/revision. Saving a next step leaves it active. Closure
records author choice, not understanding. Lounge cards remain visible to housemates.

## Alternatives and costs

All-shared permits agreed team changes, but also replacing individual statements
under the creator's label. Author-only canvas objects protect content but block
peer corrections. Delegation/shared tasks may be preferable with explicit actor/
responsibility labels and new contracts/UI.

Current policy costs author mediation for agreed transcription; it is not a general
group task manager. Familiar peers may negotiate authorship without hard rules:
research supplies that countercase, not a blanket endorsement.
