# ADR0004: a specific peer-help purpose and launch cut line

6 October 2026. Planning decision;value validation pending.

## Context and alternatives

Owner reopened purpose after the technically reviewed shared-house plan. Its
technical detail did not establish target demand or explain why users would choose
it over current products. Compare four jobs under a fixed qualitative protocol:
A start/finish alongside friends;B find a willing familiar peer when stuck;
C creative feedback;D ambient friend encounters. All owner-core game experiences
remain constraints. Current gogh/Virtual Cottage/On-Together/Gather/Discord overlap
rules out feature-absence claims.

## Decision and cost

Choose B provisionally: related-course university friends in overlapping evening
self-study;the requester voluntarily discusses a small obstacle and records their
own next step. Two fresh opposite-order reviews support this as a specific,
falsifiable task. They do not validate demand or preference. Parent keeps the
strong Discord baseline and explicit pivot criteria.

Keep2-6house code/permanent membership,own DIY bedrooms/doors,controlled avatars,
real dialogue/presence,shared seats/study and a useful live card board.
Bound furniture to six categories/ten pieces and board to one active card/member
plus twelve recent inactive(closed/departed)cards. Defer timer,pen/uploads/rich board,large catalogue
and full archive UI. Safety,private access,recovery and original archived ownership
remain protected duties,not differentiated product features.

The cost is narrower expression and fewer auxiliary tools at launch. Avatar/room
value is still a judged experiential preference;we do not claim it causes learning
gain. A pilot may show movement adds burden or questions rarely occur.

## Execution and verification

Use integrated vertical phases and explicit module interfaces. Real two-browser
movement/chat appears in S1;private visits/limited DIY in S2;question/next-step
board in S3;hardening/value comparison in S4. Chat/identity persistence is S1,
not a prematurely complete board milestone. Budget includes integration/review
and reserve rather than treating agent estimates as measured time.

[Positioning comparison](../planning/POSITIONING-COMPARISON.md),
[product purpose](../product/PRODUCT-POSITIONING.md) and root PLAN.md define
tests/falsifiers. Qualitative model evaluations ran;human baseline trials did not.
If evidence rejects B,register a candidate-specific task before testing a pivot.
Historical timer/pen policies are references;their existence cannot expand scope.
