# Proposed human check: does personal-card authority help?

Status: NOT RUN; not part of the completed technical comparison.

Question: can familiar study partners distinguish shared explanation from personal
commitment without the author restriction obstructing help?

Use 4–6 consenting pairs of university friends as a formative convenience sample,
not a representative efficacy study. Recruitment/messages need owner authorization.
Explain retained data; use practice problems, not private course work.

Each pair completes matched unfamiliar mini-problems, with order/problem assignment
counterbalanced:

- A: current shared canvas and author-controlled card.
- B: same canvas/layout, with a peer proposal to the card that its author accepts.
  This delegation prototype is NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Fix instructions, exposure time, capacity, device class and problem difficulty
where feasible. Alternate asker/helper. Before the task, ask who can edit a
diagram, close a card, and what “closed” means. Then request agreed helper
transcription. Do not reward guessing the preferred policy.

Record completion, authority misunderstandings, blocked handoffs, author mediation,
and whether people can identify who chose the final next step. Gather reasons
and requests for delegation. Assess felt control and friction separately;
satisfaction alone does not establish learning.

Pre-recruitment decision rule: retain default if participants understand the
boundary and complete individual-study tasks without recurring blocking handoffs.
If at least two pairs repeatedly need agreed peer transcription or misread creator
attribution, prioritize explicit delegation/actor labels and retest. This is a
practical formative trigger, not a population or statistical threshold.

If collective assignments dominate, revisit purpose and introduce jointly owned
tasks. If no author-boundary benefit appears and negotiated social ownership
suffices, reconsider the hard rule rather than declaring it validated.

Separately, compare the overall product against fairly configured Discord:
availability conventions, a question thread and written next step. That tests
whether the house earns its complexity, not this within-product authority policy.
